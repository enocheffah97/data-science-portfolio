# Superstore Sales & Profitability Dashboard

## Overview

**Author:** Enoch Effah | **Date:** May 2026

Interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing $2.3M in retail sales
across 4 regions and 3 product categories (2014-2017).

## Business Questions

- Which product category generates the most profit?
- Which region is most and least profitable?
- Does discounting hurt profit?
- How have sales trended over time?

## Key Findings

- Furniture generates $740K in sales but near-zero profit
- Central region has worst margin at 7.92%
- Orders with 40%+ discounts consistently generate losses
- Technology leads all categories in profitability
